    <div class="pub-item">
      <p class="pub-title"><a href="https://www.bu.edu/academics/cas/courses/cas-ee-545/">EE 545: Methods for Environmental Policy Analysis</a></p>
      <p class="pub-authors">
        Teaching Fellow, Boston University — Spring 2026
        <span class="pub-status">(instructor: Dr. Christoph Nolte)</span>
      </p>
      <p class="pub-desc">
        A rigorous introduction to the economic analysis of environmental policy, and to the
        implications of the special character of environmental problems for public decision
        making. The course introduces the tools available to environmental policy makers and
        develops quantitative frameworks for analyzing their effectiveness, advantages and
        disadvantages.
      </p>

    <div class="pub-item">
      <p class="pub-title"><a href="https://aae.wisc.edu/courses/">AAE 319: The International Agricultural Economy</a></p>
      <p class="pub-authors">
        Supplemental Instructor and Grader, University of Wisconsin–Madison — Fall 2022
        <span class="pub-status">(instructor: Dr. Charles Nicholson)</span>
      </p>
      <p class="pub-desc">
        The nature of trade in agricultural products; trade policies and practices of importing
        and exporting nations; agricultural policies of major trading blocs; market instability
        and other primary commodity problems; recent history and current developments in
        multilateral trade policy.
      </p>
      <p class="pub-note">
        Offered alternating fall semesters, 3 credits. Prerequisites: AAE 215, ECON 101 or 111.
      </p>
